# Roadmap (one phase at a time)

0. **Foundations** (in progress, see [phase-0-summary](phase-0-summary.md)): solution skeleton, CI, Docker, tenancy, auth, audit, outbox, storage/queue abstractions, i18n/RTL shell, deployment-mode switch, tenant-isolation tests.
1. **Core & Assets** (done; see [phase-1-summary](phase-1-summary.md)): org/site/building/space hierarchy, asset register, QR tags, custom fields, documents, meters.
2. **Helpdesk & Work Orders** (2a requests and lifecycle done, see [phase-2a-summary](phase-2a-summary.md); 2b SLA and notifications done, see [phase-2b-summary](phase-2b-summary.md); 2c checklists, time, costs, signatures and photos done, see [phase-2c-summary](phase-2c-summary.md); 2d technician PWA with offline sync done, see [phase-2d-summary](phase-2d-summary.md)).
3. **Preventive Maintenance** (done; see [phase-3-summary](phase-3-summary.md)): templates, schedules (fixed or floating), meter triggers, forecast calendar, compliance KPIs.
4. **Inventory** (done; see [phase-4-summary](phase-4-summary.md)): stores, stock, reservations on WOs, reorder, stock take.
5. **Vendors & Contracts** (done; see [phase-5-summary](phase-5-summary.md)): vendors, AMC/SLA contracts, purchase requests/orders, vendor portal, accounting connection (interface and fake adapter; Dynamics 365 adapter deferred until a sandbox exists).
6. **Spaces, Bookings, Visitors** (done; see [phase-6-summary](phase-6-summary.md)): floor plans with pins, allocation and occupancy, bookable resources with rules and approval, calendar feed and a calendar connection interface, visitor pre-registration with QR pass, front desk, watchlist and badges.
7. **Reporting & Admin** (done; see [phase-7-summary](phase-7-summary.md)): fixed KPI pack with filters and CSV, read-only OData feed with revocable tokens, workflow designer (rules inside fixed safety limits, versioned), audit viewer with export, Administration hub. SLA and custom-field setup already existed from earlier phases.
8. **Packaging & Licensing** (done, with stated limits; see [phase-8-summary](phase-8-summary.md)): signed offline license with module, user and expiry enforcement, hardening and security self-assessment (no external pen test yet), Helm chart, Docker Compose on-prem bundle, Azure Bicep, release pipeline, upgrade and rollback tooling.
9. **Compliance, Energy, Projects, Leases** (done; see [phase-9-summary](phase-9-summary.md)): obligations and permits to work, energy readings with device secrets and alarms, projects with budget roll-up from work and purchasing, leases with rent schedule and deadline warnings. AI was deferred.
10. **AI assistance** (done, with stated limits; see [phase-10-summary](phase-10-summary.md)): optional, off by default, provider interface with the Claude API first; category and priority suggestions for requests, work order summaries, repeat-problem hints.
11. **Closing gaps** (done, with stated limits; see [phase-11-summary](phase-11-summary.md)): work orders from alerts, a real rehearsal of the Compose bundle (five defects found and fixed), AI feedback and duplicate hints, malware scanning, contact-detail encryption, a shared rate counter.
12. **Suggestions and field gaps** (done, with stated limits; see [phase-12-summary](phase-12-summary.md)): suggested people and vendors for a work order, early warnings from repair intervals and meter pace (rules, with their numbers), vendor contact details encrypted, permits and alert-raised jobs on the technician's phone.
13. **Later**: the gaps listed in the phase summaries (a first Helm and Azure deployment, a model behind the suggestions, encrypting lease terms, the remaining mobile gaps).

Each phase ships with tests and updated docs. Do not start the next phase until told.
